TRANSPARENT BY DESIGN

Numbers you can interrogate.

01

Exact arithmetic

Plot value = area × asking rate. Stamp duty, registration, brokerage and other costs remain separate unless explicitly added.

02

Comparable evidence

Nearby asking prices are not sale deeds. We show the sample, distance and source quality instead of presenting guesses as facts.

03

Vastu context

Facing is only one factor. Entrance position, plot shape, slope, road approach and building layout matter. Traditions vary.

04

Human verification

Government guideline values, registered transactions, title, approvals and boundaries require authoritative local checks.
